Understanding the True Cost of International Money Transfers

The true cost of an international money transfer cannot be understood by looking at a single fee alone. In practice, the total cost is determined by the combination of transfer fees, exchange rate margins, intermediary bank charges, and the final amount received.

One of the most significant hidden costs is the exchange rate margin, which often exceeds the visible transfer fee.

Traditional banks often rely on multiple intermediary institutions to settle cross-border transfers. During this process, intermediary or recipient banks may deduct additional fees without prior notice. While fintech-based transfer services reduce intermediaries and simplify pricing, exchange rate margins often remain the largest hidden cost factor.

When comparing international transfer services, three metrics matter most:

  • The total amount paid by the sender
  • The final amount received by the recipient
  • The difference between the two (the true total cost)
