How Much Does It Cost to Send $500 Internationally?

Sending $500 internationally can cost more than you expect. Banks and money transfer services often include hidden fees and exchange rate markups.

Total Cost Breakdown

  • Transfer fee: $10 – $30
  • Exchange rate margin: $5 – $25
  • Intermediary bank fees: $10 – $20

In total, sending $500 can cost between $25 and $75 depending on the provider.

Why Fees Vary

The total cost depends on how the provider makes money. Some charge upfront fees, while others hide costs in exchange rates.

Learn more about exchange rate margins.

How to Reduce Costs

  • Use services with transparent pricing
  • Avoid banks when possible
  • Compare total cost, not just fees
