About Costoria

Costoria is an educational website dedicated to helping people understand the real cost of international money transfers.

Many users focus only on transfer fees, but the true cost often includes hidden charges such as exchange rate margins and intermediary bank fees.

Our goal is to provide clear, simple explanations and practical tools so users can make better financial decisions when sending money abroad.

We aim to make international transfers more transparent by breaking down costs and providing easy-to-use resources.

If you are sending money internationally, Costoria helps you understand what you are really paying.
