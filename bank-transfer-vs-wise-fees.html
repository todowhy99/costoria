Bank Transfer vs Wise Fees

Choosing between traditional bank transfers and Wise can significantly impact your costs. Here's a detailed comparison.

1. Bank Transfer Costs

Banks charge high fees, apply less favorable exchange rates, and may include intermediary bank fees. Read about core fees here and exchange rate margins here.

2. Wise Fees

Wise shows all fees upfront, often offering better exchange rates. Use the Costoria calculator to see exact costs for your transfer.

3. When to Choose Which

If speed and convenience are critical, banks may still be used. For cost savings and transparency, Wise is usually cheaper.

4. Summary

  • Compare total costs, not just transfer fees.
  • Always check exchange rate margins before sending money.
