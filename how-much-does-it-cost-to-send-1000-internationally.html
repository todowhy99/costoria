How Much Does It Cost to Send $1000 Internationally?

Sending $1000 internationally might seem simple, but the total cost can vary depending on how you send it.

Typical Costs

  • Transfer fees
  • Exchange rate margin
  • Intermediary bank fees

Example

Using a traditional bank:

  • Transfer fee: $25
  • Exchange margin: ~3%
  • Total cost: around $55

Using a modern transfer service:

  • Lower fees
  • Closer to real exchange rate
  • Total cost: around $10–$20

How to Reduce Costs

  • Avoid traditional banks
  • Compare exchange rates
  • Use transparent services

Conclusion

Most people overpay when sending money internationally without realizing it.
